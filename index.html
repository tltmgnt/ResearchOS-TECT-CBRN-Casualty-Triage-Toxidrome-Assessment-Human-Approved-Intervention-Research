MODESIMULATION
RESEARCH LIMITThis software does not detect casualties, diagnose patients or administer treatment. Assessment labels are entered by a researcher. Human approval controls a simulation record only.

THE WORKFLOW

Assessment to review.
Review to simulated outcome.

  1. Select a synthetic case and start its assessment timer.
  2. Record a declared category, triage label and observation quality.
  3. A reviewer approves, refuses or withholds a simulation task.
  4. Record a simulated outcome while approval remains valid.
  5. Export the study and replayable event record.

04 / EVALUATION

Measure the record.
Bound the claim.

Agreement is against visible, authored synthetic labels. It is not blinded model accuracy, clinical sensitivity, or evidence of autonomous recognition.

Scripted category coverage

Hits require a matching declared category, complete quality and non-withheld triage. Unassessed or withheld authored positives count as misses. Intervals are descriptive 95% Wilson intervals; repeated template cases are not independent clinical samples.

05 / TOXIDROME REFERENCE

Trace the requirement.
Preserve the uncertainty.

Condensed from the solicitation Tables 1 and 2 supplied by the repository owner. Simulation planning only; no dosing, patient treatment protocol or chemical-agent identification.

Observation and intervention limits

Palpable pulses and auscultation are not automatically available from standoff imagery. Missing cues must remain unavailable. Specific-agent identification and chemical-specific sensors are out of scope.

The supplied airway requirement includes oxygen by mask and escalation to non-invasive or invasive ventilation. All physical tasks require separate medical, device and manikin validation. IV/IO catheter placement is a separate requirement if the blood-agent category is selected. This app implements none of those procedures.
