# Validation and readiness plan

## Software checks implemented
Node unit tests verify simulation-only input validation, state prerequisites, expiry, reviewer/rationale requirements, case binding, changed assessments, safety invalidation, refusal, one-use approvals, failed-attempt inventory, exhausted inventory, double-completion rejection, altered records, monotonic event times, replay and denominator handling. A complete five-case sequence across two category labels is exercised as software simulation.

CLI checks initialize a portable record, replay it, generate research metrics and export CSV. Browser rendering and keyboard/mobile usability require separate interactive checks. Passing tests does not establish clinical, hardware or regulatory readiness.

## Evidence required outside this app
The owner's supplied topic requires prior baseline evidence, including localization within 2 m, trauma triage, accurate standoff heart/respiratory rates, at least 20 casualties, per-casualty assessment under 90 seconds, and 30,000 sq ft surveyed without battery change. These remain unverified. Indoor/outdoor, day/night, portability and terrain-transition demonstrations also need actual artifacts.

At least two toxidrome categories and selected physical interventions must be demonstrated on high-fidelity manikins under human treatment decisions. Airway tasks cannot be replaced by a checkbox. Final five-casualty physical delivery without battery change/resupply and required real-terrain work remain unimplemented hardware requirements.

## Evaluation protocol to establish separately
Use a qualified clinical panel and an independent evaluation lead. Separate training/tuning/locked test sets by scenario and acquisition session. Record observability, absent versus unmeasured cues, mixed presentations, trauma, mimics, abstention and disagreement. Report confusion matrices, timing definitions, confidence intervals, all exclusions and failure cases. Predefine sample sizes and acceptance limits with clinical and sponsor review.

Test stale identity, communications interruption, payload mismatch, contact uncertainty, power failure and incomplete delivery on instrumented manikins before any further physical study. Clinical safety, human-subject protections, device authorization and real casualty outcomes require separate qualified review. No claim of survival improvement follows from synthetic simulations.
